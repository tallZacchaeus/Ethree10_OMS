# Team Member — user guide

You deliver the work. Your app is deliberately small: ten menu items, and most
days you will use three of them.

## When you are given something

You get an **email the moment work is assigned to you**, containing:

- what the task is, and the brief
- which project and department it belongs to
- its priority and deadline
- the estimated effort
- the acceptance criteria — what "done" means
- a direct link to open it

You do not need to check the app to find out what you have been given.

## Your screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **My Work** | Everything assigned to you, grouped by urgency. Your home. |
| **My Contributions** | Your traceable record — completed work, deliverables, reviews, hours. This is what feeds your reports. |
| **Requests / Projects** | Context on the work your branch is doing. |
| **Notifications** | Assignments, mentions, review outcomes. |
| **My Profile** | Your timezone and weekly capacity. Your role, branch, department and skills are set by leadership. |

## Doing a task

1. Open it from **My Work** or from the email link.
2. Move it to *in progress* when you start.
3. Log time as you go — this is what makes **Workload** honest for everyone, and
   it feeds your contribution record.
4. Attach deliverables and versions as you produce them.
5. **Submit for review** with a summary of what you did, evidence or a link, and
   the hours spent.

Your department lead reviews it. They either accept it, or send it back with
revisions and a reason. Revisions are normal and are recorded as part of the work
history.

## What you cannot do

You cannot assign work, route requests, approve anything, or see money. If you
need a task reassigned or the scope changed, comment on it or speak to your
department lead.

## A note on clients

Clients do not have accounts. They submit a request from the public site and get a
private **tracking link** that shows status and lets them talk to the team. When
you or a lead reply to a client on a request, choose carefully between an internal
note and a client-visible reply — client-visible replies appear on their link.
