# Department Lead — user guide

You lead one department inside a branch — for example *Engineering* inside **Tech
& Product**, or *Video & Photography* inside **Digital Media**. You report to your
branch head.

## What you do here

- **Assign and re-assign** your department's work.
- **Review** what your people deliver before it goes up for final approval.
- **Move requests along** their lifecycle within your department's remit.
- **Create and update projects** your department owns.
- **Request spend** against a project's approved budget.

## What you deliberately cannot do

You cannot restructure the branch, create or archive departments, or change the
service catalogue — that is your branch head. You cannot approve budgets (Chief
Executive) or pay anything (Finance).

## Day to day

Your home is the **Branch Dashboard** and the delivery screens beneath it:

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Assignments** | Who on your team is accountable for what. |
| **Workload** | Real capacity — estimates against hours, with leave and blockers. |
| **Reviews** | Work submitted by your people, awaiting your decision. |
| **My Work** | Anything assigned to you personally. |
| **Expenses** | Spend requests against approved budgets. |

## Assigning work

Assign from the task, or from **Assignments**. The moment you assign someone, they
receive an email containing the task title, project, department, priority,
deadline, estimated effort, the brief, and the acceptance criteria — with a direct
link to the task.

Use **Workload** before assigning. It is calculated from real estimates and logged
hours, not from anyone's self-report, so it is a reliable picture of who actually
has room.

## Reviewing work

When someone submits a task for completion they include a summary, evidence and
hours. You either accept it or send it back with revisions. Revisions are
recorded — the history is visible and auditable, which is the point.

## Money

You can request spend against a project whose budget the Chief Executive has
approved. If the budget is not approved yet, the request is blocked and you will
be told why. If your request would push committed spend past the approved
internal amount, it is blocked and the system shows you both figures.

You cannot pay a request — Finance does that, and never the person who raised it.
