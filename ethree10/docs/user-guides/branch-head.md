# Branch Head — user guide

You head one of the agency's two branches — **Digital Media** or **Tech &
Product** — and every department inside it. You report to the Chief Executive.

## What you do here

- **Accept and route work** that arrives for your branch.
- **Create and manage departments** inside your branch, and assign each a lead
  and members.
- **Assign work** to the right people, based on skill and current load.
- **Review and approve** delivered work before it reaches the client.
- **Submit budgets** for your projects, for the Chief Executive to approve.
- **Request spend** against approved budgets.

## What you deliberately cannot do

You cannot approve your own budget, or anyone's. Budgets go to the Chief
Executive. If you try to approve one, you are blocked — including your own
submissions. You also cannot confirm payments or pay expenses; that is Finance.

## Triage — the step that matters most

Client requests now arrive **unclassified**. The requester describes what they
need in their own words; they do not pick a service, set an urgency, or write
acceptance criteria. That is your job, and it is where the quality of everything
downstream is decided.

From **Intake Queue** and **Brief Review** you:

1. Read what the client actually asked for.
2. Set the **service** — which also routes it to the right branch.
3. Set the **urgency**.
4. Agree the scope with the client in the request's conversation thread. The
   client sees your client-visible replies on their tracking link; internal notes
   stay hidden.
5. Accept it, request clarification, reject it, or re-route it to the other
   branch.

Because requests no longer route themselves, the Intake Queue is a **daily**
step, not an exception queue.

## Getting a project funded

1. Create the project from the accepted request.
2. Submit its budget: the total, what the client is billed, and what you expect to
   spend internally.
3. The Chief Executive approves or rejects it.
4. Once approved, Finance can invoice, and you can request spend.

If you need more money later, resubmit. The budget returns to *awaiting approval*
and the previous approval is cleared — a raised ceiling always needs a fresh
decision.

## Assigning work

When you assign a task, the assignee is emailed immediately with the task title,
the project it belongs to, its department, priority, deadline, estimated effort,
what needs doing, and the acceptance criteria — plus a direct link. They do not
have to open the app to know what they have been given.

Re-saving a task does not re-notify. Only an actual change of hands does.

## Your screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Branch Dashboard** | Your branch's control surface. |
| **Intake Queue / Brief Review** | Classify, route and accept incoming work. |
| **Assignments** | Who is accountable for what. |
| **Workload** | Capacity from real assignments, estimates and leave. |
| **Reviews** | Approve work or send it back with auditable revisions. |
| **Expenses** | Request spend against approved budgets. |
| **Branches / People** | Departments, leads and members. |
| **Service Catalog** | The services your branch offers, with routing and SLA. |
